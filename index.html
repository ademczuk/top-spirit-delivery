The delivery plan

Four gated phases, proven before each next step

Each gate is a decision point you control. Stage-gate governance (PMI / PRINCE2) protects your budget on the outside; the pilot runs in agile sprints inside, a hybrid model. The durations are typical for a company your size and set with you in discovery, not fixed here.

Safe by design

Every task runs the same safe loop

AI literacy rollout

Connected, not siloed

What one team learns reaches the others. The champion network is a Kotter guiding coalition; adoption follows the Prosci ADKAR model, awareness through to reinforcement. One trained champion per department, a shared playbook, a weekly open hour, so the learning keeps moving after we step back.
